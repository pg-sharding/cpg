Subject: GIN scan experiments: scope relative to the build/extraction work

Hi David,

I have been comparing a separate set of GIN scan experiments with this
thread, including v12. The older trigram radix, MSD and adaptive-sort
experiments are follow-ups to your extraction work. The typed positional
trigram sort is related too, although it sorts pos_trgm with an additional
position tie-break.

The current experiments operate later: advancing decoded posting lists,
decoding private compressed snapshots on demand, selecting required entry
streams with a heap, and memoizing the reviewed builtin tsquery ternary
callback. Your accumulator and extraction patches do not implement these
scan paths. The heap, logical TID, consistent-cache and deferred-decode
ideas already have direct prior discussion in Heikki's 2014 message [1];
the contribution here is the concrete contracts and reproducible positive
and negative evidence, without an algorithmic novelty claim.

Our historical B72 baseline already includes the inline comparison,
April sort/dedup commits and November integer-comparator change. We have
not measured a combined reader/builder patch set, so the scan results
should not be added to the CREATE INDEX speedups in this thread.
