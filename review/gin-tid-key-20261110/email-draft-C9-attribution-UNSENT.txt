Subject: [PATCH] Reduce GIN posting-list advancement and startup decoding costs
Status: UNSENT DRAFT; attribution updated 2026-11-10; numbers remain the historical R3 measurements

Hi,

Attached is a prototype combining adaptive advancement in decoded GIN TID lists with deferred consumption of private compressed posting-page snapshots. It follows up on the separate advance V4 / lazy V4 experiments. The aim here is to retain their selective-query gains while removing the observed tiny-inline and absent-query costs.

There are three parts: compare logical unsigned TID keys directly when only ordering is needed; use two cheap probes before exponential/binary upper-bound advancement; and defer compressed first-page materialization for multi-entry scans. For the last part, counting varbyte terminators with masked popcount gives the exact original first-page cardinality, preserving the frequency estimate and entry ordering/sampling thresholds. Singleton, uncompressed and empty startup paths remain eager. Existing ownership and buffer-pin rules are retained; no new on-disk format is introduced.

New full-backend SQL results on fixed B72 plus the same common prerequisite in every tree are below. Numbers are cohort 0 / cohort 1; the two CPU/slot/session/case-order cohorts are kept separate. A separate real-AM C adapter loops over the actual index_rescan/index_getbitmap/tbm_free path, compiled against the installed server public headers. Its internal process-CPU medians show combined/B -17.89 / -17.05% for tiny inline and -87.41 / -88.04% for absent. These are not heap-visibility or SQL-throughput measurements. The native and SQL boundaries are reported separately.

Full SQL still has positive OR and single-rare CPU medians versus B in both cohorts. The second cohort is near/under the exact twin, but this is not normalized away and the prototype is not ready to merge without resolving or bounding those costs. A separate real-AM OR/rare diagnostic follows this frozen packet.

Negative experiments are included: shrinking private scan-entry state (V7) did not repair inline. Boolean ordering alone (L8), and first-page deferral without adaptive advancement (L9), still had native inline costs; L9 also had repeated OR costs. The combined candidate is measured directly, not inferred by adding gains from alternatives. The screen had OR/Seq increases in one cohort that reversed in the other. All full-SQL controls, baseline twins, ranges and per-round values are retained; no samples are trimmed, pooled or normalized by the twin. Earlier off-path Seq movements remain historical unresolved observations, not proof of an algorithmic cause or harmless noise.

Setup: VM158, GCC 13.3, -O2 -g -fno-lto, OpenSSL, no ICU/readline, release timing builds, JIT/parallel/memoize/autovacuum off, 512MB shared_buffers and warm-cache synthetic data (200000 generated docs plus NULL/empty rows and a small inline fixture). Full SQL uses 25 unchanged queries, two warm and 12 retained balanced rounds per role in each cohort. Exact data/index definitions, build flags/revisions, dependencies and commands for repeating both the real-AM and SQL tests are in REPRODUCE.md; the packet includes raw observations, absolute ranges, SD/MAD and every pair. No CREATE INDEX or current-master result is claimed.

All builds passed the original 239 tests, including an assertion build. Ordinary ordered Seq/GIN multisets, prepared rescans, expected paths and AM answers matched. Assertions independently check first-page cardinality by decoding. The executed source, object/header identities and clean owned-cluster shutdowns are recorded. The bundled diff was reapplied to the exact fixed baseline and matched the measured source.

Previous discussion: Heikki explicitly proposed both integer TID comparisons and private compressed-segment copying with deferred decoding in 2014 [5]. This is a direct conceptual predecessor to the lazy-consumption part of this prototype. The earlier fast-scan work [1,2] already introduced advancePast and compressed segment skipping. Galloping search, boolean ordering and lazy consumption are established techniques. The contribution is their GIN integration, exact first-page counting and measured tradeoffs, not a priority claim. The extraction/sort/build-accumulator work [3,4] concerns an earlier phase; its gains are not mixed with these consumer measurements. The bounded prior-art review and negative results are included.

Candidate: patches/gin-lazy-adaptive-v9-over-fixed-b72.diff (after fixed base 8a8c475037bfde101da23cc9b0364c9c020eca93). Incremental format-patches and complete B72 source plans are also included; these are alternative forms, not patches to apply twice.
