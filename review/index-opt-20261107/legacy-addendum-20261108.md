# Historical B-tree source addendum — 8 November 2026

This bounded inventory covers our earlier production prototypes in six B-tree review roots. It adds 17 missing source alternatives and reuses 7 exact equivalent published trees, for 24 distinct historical trees. Every new source commit has the pinned upstream base as its sole parent. These are alternatives, not a patch series. No builds or new performance measurements were run for publication.

Failed, superseded, mixed and micro-only alternatives are retained with their historical evidence scope; publication does not recommend merging them. Full negative results remain in the historical internal artifact bundles. The current 31-variant source index is unchanged.

One historical typed-v1 control is archived byte-for-byte with 30 Git indent-with-spaces warnings. Its strict whitespace check did not pass; plain apply, source provenance and code-only checks did. Formatting was not silently corrected.
