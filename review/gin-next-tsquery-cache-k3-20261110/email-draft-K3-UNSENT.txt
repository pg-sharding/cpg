Subject: [DRAFT UNSENT] Builtin tsquery GIN memo experiment: gains and remaining regressions

Hi,

I tried a bounded last-vector memo for builtin tsquery GIN consistency,
including a fixed fallback after64 consecutive vector misses per scan key.
A hit resets the counter; miss64 computes the original native result once
and selects the direct helper for the remaining key lifetime. This is a
standalone alternative over fixed historical B72, not a series with K1 or
other GIN changes. The common supplied prerequisite is identical in all trees.

The memo requires the reviewed builtin support OIDs and native function
addresses, keeps at most eight ternary entries and preserves FALSE/TRUE/MAYBE
and native recheck behavior. Arbitrary opclasses/shims retain their callbacks;
hidden-entry growth beyond the bound falls back. A small per-key allocation
remains. No on-disk format or callback API changes. The threshold was declared
before this campaign and was not retuned from its observations.

Four clean B/K1/K3/K3_assert builds passed unchanged core239 and posting-list
tests. The ordinary/native/fallback gate passed160 ordered SQL,28 prepared,
48 lifecycle and44 generic-fallback cases,735888 exhaustive native checks,
and51744 new budget checks including180 dispatch transitions. A separately
reviewed92-command smoke preceded the fixed four-role B/T/K1/K3 comparison.
T is the exact B executable and entrypoint. All2576 raw /2208 retained /
368 warm observations,23 SQL/actual-AM cells and all six pairs are preserved
in two separate fresh cohorts with12 retained rounds. Independent arithmetic
matched79040 numerical fields;200 ordered Seq/GIN oracles and all checked
plan trees/rows/loops agreed. All17 owned cluster lifetimes stopped cleanly.

Observed same-round paired medians, cohort0 /cohort1:

* Dense SQL executor: K3/K1 -5.71% /-5.63%, but K3/B still +2.62% /+3.02%.
  Absolute B/K1/K3 medians were82.767/90.076/84.365ms and
  81.534/89.735/84.260ms for10 probes. This reduces rather than removes
  the regression.
* OR SQL executor: K3/B -4.25% /-3.01%; B/K3 medians151.071/143.999ms
  and150.154/146.062ms for5 probes. Full single-term SQL was mixed:
  -2.24% /+0.65%, with144.123/140.971ms and144.831/145.825ms.
* Full actual-AM inner CPU: K3/B -15.30% /-6.54%; B/K3 medians
  107.338/90.932ms and96.943/90.594ms for10 complete scans.
  OR inner CPU: -21.83% /-13.53%,122.298/95.653ms and110.328/95.416ms.
  K3/K1 changes in those AM cells were small, about +0.06..+0.32%.
* Dense actual-AM inner CPU remains negative against T: +7.79% /+9.17%.
  Rare-single SQL executor K3/K1 regressed +7.99% /+5.10%, with all12

  The unchanged Seq executor control K3/B was +0.23% /+2.01%.

VM158 had an own quiet window without our compilers/profiles; no hardware
isolation is claimed. Exact B/T controls still varied materially in cohort0:
AM OR T/B -9.50%, dense -6.61%; cohort1 was +0.21% /+0.29% respectively.
These observations do not establish source-level causes. Both cohorts and
all controls remain without trimming, pooling, twin normalization or
significance claims. Executor time, whole-command backend CPU/client wall,
and AM inner CPU/wall have separate boundaries. A separate literal execution
before each sample adds warming. Retained backend reads/writes and minor/
major faults were zero; total runqueue deltas34.31/26.00ms are diagnostics.
Complete min/median/max, mean, sample SD, MAD, raw arrays and direct pairs
are included, rather than only these selected summaries.

Consistent-result caching was already proposed by Heikki in2014[1], following
earlier fast-scan discussion[2]. No algorithmic novelty is claimed for caching
or adaptive fallback. The concrete work here is the builtin-scoped prototype,
bounded fallback and reproducible qualification/negative performance evidence.
It is not ready as a general upgrade; the remaining dense/rare/prefix costs
need explanation. Earlier K1 shared-load VM84 results remain separate.

The packet includes standalone .diff and root-authored format-patch source,
exact revisions/toolchain flags and synthetic setup, SQL/AM harness, all raw
observations, offline statistic replay and independent checks. A new portable
stage frontdoor is AST/help/source checked only; independent Linux replay via
that frontdoor has not been executed. The original controllers did execute.
