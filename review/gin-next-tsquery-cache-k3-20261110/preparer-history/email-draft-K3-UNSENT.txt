Subject: [DRAFT UNSENT] Bounded last-vector memo for builtin tsquery consistency

This is an independently applicable prototype over the fixed historical B72
baseline, not a patch series with the earlier memo or other GIN experiments.
It retains the exact builtin-support OID/native-address guards and ternary/
recheck behavior, and stops memo lookup after64 consecutive vector misses per
scan key. A memo hit resets the count. The64th miss computes the ordinary
native result once, then selects the original direct helper until that scan
key is rebuilt. A small query-local memo allocation remains. The threshold
was fixed before measurements; there is no on-disk or callback API change.

The motivation is the earlier K1 screen's no-reuse dense negative. On its
separate shared-load VM84 cohorts, K1 increased actual-AM inner CPU by13.63%
and13.94%; those results and controls remain immutable. Aggregate high OR
repeat rates do not prove short miss runs, so preserved OR benefit is a
hypothesis, not a source-review conclusion. A long no-reuse prefix can still
discard later hits permanently. Short scans and hit-reset overhead are controls.

Four fresh B/K1/K3/K3_assert full builds passed unchanged core239 and posting
list tests with actual source/header/compiler/ELF bindings. The installed gate
passed160 ordered SQL,28 prepared,48 lifecycle and44 generic fallback cases,
735888 exhaustive native checks and51744 new budget dispatch checks. The latter
observed180 pointer transitions,528 initializations and88 key-context resets;
this context-reset helper is not labelled an index_rescan execution. The
separate actual-AM helper uses the real scan/rescan/bitmap path. All five gate
cluster lifetimes stopped cleanly. The92-observation zero-accepted smoke and
100 ordered oracles passed independent semantic review.

The prospective matched matrix is B/exact-ELF twin/K1/K3,23 cells, two fresh
cohorts and12 retained rounds, keeping all controls and every pair. Complete
accepted timings,independent statistics and final live release are not yet
part of this preparer snapshot; no speedup,dense-fix or adoption claim is made.
The final draft must insert the verified complete tables and negative findings

K3 is a small builtin-scoped last-vector experiment with a fixed fallback
budget; no general novelty claim is made for memoization or adaptive fallback.
